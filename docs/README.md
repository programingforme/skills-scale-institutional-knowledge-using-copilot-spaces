# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides an overview of OctoAcme's project management processes and links to all detailed documentation. It is intended as an entry point for both new and existing team members.

## Summary of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme's project management processes are grounded in clear workflows, well-defined roles, and a commitment to collaboration and continuous improvement. Projects follow a deliberate lifecycle that begins with initiation—clarifying business needs, defining measurable outcomes, and aligning stakeholders. This flows into planning, where the team breaks down work into shippable increments, identifies dependencies and risks, and establishes concrete milestones. Execution is managed through structured rhythms like daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and regular demos, ensuring that progress, challenges, and learnings are surfaced early and often. Each phase culminates in release and retrospective activities, where the team deploys updates, verifies outcomes, and captures insights for continuous improvement.

Key personas drive this process: Developers focus on delivering reliable, maintainable code and participate in design and testing; Product Managers own the vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure value through customer outcomes and metrics; and Project Managers coordinate delivery, manage risk, and ensure alignment across stakeholders. Each role has clearly documented responsibilities, communication expectations, and goals, which are detailed in the process documentation to ensure accountability and clarity.

Communication is foundational at OctoAcme. Teams adopt regular cadences such as daily standups, weekly syncs, and monthly stakeholder updates, with ad-hoc escalation paths for urgent issues. Project Managers are responsible for maintaining up-to-date documentation, risk registers, and frequent status reports, while Product Managers keep stakeholders informed on roadmap and priorities. The use of centralized artifacts—like the project one-pager, risk register, and release notes—guarantees a single source of truth throughout the project lifecycle.

Quality assurance is woven into every process step. All PRs are required to include issue links and acceptance criteria, automated tests must pass before merging, and manual QA or stakeholder reviews are mandated for key releases. Pre-release requirements, deployment checklists, and clear rollback playbooks help minimize risk and support dependable delivery. After each release or milestone, the team conducts structured retrospectives, tracks actionable improvements, and celebrates progress, ensuring a culture of learning and continuous enhancement.
